About Me

I am a robotics engineering student at Purdue University focused on designing the electrical and control architectures that enable intelligent machines to operate reliably and efficiently. My work centers on embedded systems, motor control, sensor fusion, and real-time integration, building the foundational infrastructure that connects hardware to autonomy.


I am motivated by systems-level engineering challenges where performance, scalability, and precision matter. Rather than building isolated features, I aim to develop cohesive hardware–software platforms that can support increasingly complex capabilities over time.


My long-term goal is to contribute to next-generation hardware and robotic systems that define how intelligent technology interacts with the physical world.
