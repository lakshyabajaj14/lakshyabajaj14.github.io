About Me

I'm a robotics engineering student at Purdue focused on the hardware and control layers that let robots sense and act in the physical world, from custom PCB-based tactile sensor arrays to embedded control systems and motor-level integration.

I like getting close to the hardware: building the sensing and signal pipelines that feed into higher-level autonomy, and increasingly, the imitation-learning and ROS2 systems that turn that sensor data into robot behavior.

My long-term goal is to work on robots that can perceive and manipulate the physical world reliably, combining tactile sensing, embedded systems, and learning-based control.
