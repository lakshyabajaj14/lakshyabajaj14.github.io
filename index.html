About

I'm a Robotics Engineering Technology student at Purdue, concentrating in Autonomy and UXVs. I started at the hardware end, with PCBs, sensors and motor drivers, and have been working up the stack into ROS 2 and imitation learning.

Right now I'm researching vision-language-action models for manipulation. What I care about most is robots that stay useful when the policy gets it wrong: noticing the failure and handing off cleanly.
